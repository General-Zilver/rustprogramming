Source Document 02: Character Profiles & Dynamics
I. The Protagonist
Name: [[Zilver]] (Alias: Merlin)
Archetype: The Unconscious Saint / The Architect.
Affinity: Universal (Saint Variance).
Note: He is a "Prism." He has no internal filter, allowing him to access all frequencies (Heat, Time, Bio, Force) with 1x Cost, provided his intent is altruistic.
The Mechanic: Subconscious Calculation ("Fool's Luck").
Zilver does not "cast" spells consciously (yet). His brain analyzes a problem and the Ethos Field edits reality to fit his solution.
Visual: He enters a Synaptic Lock (The Trance). He freezes, pupils dilate, breathing stops.
The Cost: Massive calorie depletion. He suffers from acute hypoglycemia (The Crash) after every major feat.
Motivation: To prove his innocence, save his friends, and solve the ultimate puzzle (The Origin of Magic).
II. The Antagonist
Name: [[High Steward Cygnus]]
Archetype: The Tyrant / The Dark Mirror.
Affinity: Universal (Tyrant Variance).
The Mechanic: Parasitic Conductance.
Cygnus forces the Ethos Field to obey. To bypass the fatal Synaptic Load of high-level magic, he uses "Batteries" (Cultists) to shoulder the cost.
Visual: Wears pristine White and Silver. Seemingly ageless. When he casts, people around him get nosebleeds or faint.
The Goal: To rewrite history. He believes the world is flawed and he is the only one with the "Vision" to fix it (by becoming King/God).
III. The Crew (The Specialists)
These characters represent the specific Affinities (Colors of Magic). They are "Citizens" with high potential in one specific area.
1. Elara (The Mute / "Mouse")
Affinity: Biomatics (Green Logic).
Heist Role: The Radar / Surveillance.
Mechanic: Sensory Bonding. She bonds with small animals (rats/birds) to see through their eyes. She can read the micro-expressions and heart rates of guards to detect lies or patrols.
Trait: Mute due to trauma. Extremely expressive eyes. Begins malnourished/dirty, reveals a unique beauty as she heals. Eventually speaks only critical intel.
Future Title: The Origin of Bonding.
2. Kael (The Outcast)
Affinity: Chrono-Kinetics (Violet Logic).
Heist Role: The Ghost / Infiltration.
Mechanic: Temporal Skipping. He doesn't turn invisible; he steps "between" seconds. He exists in the blind spots of human perception.
Trait: Asian descent, treated as invisible by society. Uses this social invisibility to fuel his magical invisibility. Quiet, efficient, cynical.
Future Title: The Origin of Time.
3. Mara (The Mother)
Affinity: Thermodynamics (Red Logic).
Heist Role: The Distraction / Demolitions.
Mechanic: Thermal Manipulation. She can boil a pot of soup instantly (Hearth) or melt a lock/cause a black powder keg to ignite (Fury). Her body temperature runs naturally high.
Trait: Voluptuous, protective, maternal. Not an outcast, but "othered." Fiercely defends the crew.
Future Title: The Origin of Energy.
4. Silas (The Monk)
Affinity: Kinetic Reinforcement (Gold Logic).
Heist Role: The Shield / Muscle.
Mechanic: Density Shifting. He can harden specific patches of skin to the density of iron to block blows. He can increase his mass to become an immovable object.
Trait: Elderly Black man, fit physique, shaved head (white hair). Gentle personality, violent capability. Meditates to manage his Synaptic Load.
Future Title: The Origin of Force.
IV. The Supporting Cast
1. Dirk
Role: The Anchor.
Function: Zilver’s connection to the mundane world. Dirk is not magic. He reminds the reader (and Zilver) of the stakes for normal people. He provides the resources/hideouts initially.
2. Melanie
Role: The Healer (Traditional).
Function: Represents "Old Medicine" vs. Zilver's "New Magic." She fixes the physical damage Zilver does to himself. A grounded, wise mother figure.
3. King Daveon
Role: The Puppet.
Function: A illiterate, boisterous King who is easily manipulated by Cygnus. He isn't evil, just incompetent and obsessed with his legacy (The Obelisk).
