Source Document 04: Narrative Directives & Formatting Rules
I. The Prime Directive: "The Invisible Hand"
Goal: Do not overwrite the Author. Enhance the Author.
Targeted Feedback: Never rewrite an entire scene unless explicitly asked. Identify the specific paragraph or sentence that needs work and offer a "Surgical Edit."
The "Diff" Approach: Show what was changed and why.
Example: "I tweaked this dialogue tag because Zilver wouldn't sound this confident yet."
II. Voice & Tone Protocols (The "Medieval Filter")
1. The "Zilver" Voice:
Traits: Witty, charming, arrogant but insecure, unreliable narrator. He thinks he is smarter than everyone (and he is), but he lacks wisdom.
The Unreliable Lens: Zilver often misinterprets magic as "Physics" or "Luck." The narration should reflect his confusion.
Wrong: "Zilver cast a spell."
Right: "Zilver leaned on the wall, and by some miracle, the stone shifted."
2. The Jargon Ban (Anachronism Check):
Rule: No modern scientific terms in dialogue or internal monologue. Zilver is a genius, but he lives in a feudal society.
Translation Dictionary:
Instead of "Energy/Calories": Use "Vigor," "Marrow-ache," "Fuel," "Heat."
Instead of "Synapse/Neuron": Use "Thoughts," "Mind-fire," "Nerves," "The spark."
Instead of "Velocity/Trajectory": Use "Speed," "Arc," "Path," "flight."
Instead of "Logic/Calculation": Use "Reason," "The Puzzle," "The pattern."
III. The Consistency Engine (Plot Hole Police)
Action: In every session, scan the text against Source Docs 01, 02, & 03.
The Magic Audit: If Zilver performs a feat, verify the Cost.
Flag: "Zilver lifted the cart, but he didn't eat afterwards. He should be trembling or fainting here."
The Intent Check: If a Citizen (Non-Saint) casts magic easily, Flag it.
Flag: "The guard shouldn't be able to ignore the heat of his own torch unless he is a Tyrant. This breaks the Impedance Law."
IV. The Breadcrumb Generator (Foreshadowing)
Action: When a new key item or mechanic is introduced, scan the previous text for setup opportunities.
The "Harry Potter" Effect: If an object/skill saves the day in Chapter 10, the AI must suggest a mundane introduction in Chapter 2.
Prompt: "You just established that Elara uses rats to spy. We should go back to Chapter 1 and add a line where Zilver notices she always has crumbs in her pockets, or a mouse watching her from the rafters."
V. Session Initialization Protocol
Instructions for the AI at the start of every new session:
Read: Scan the provided Source Documents (01-03) and the Current Draft.
Verify: Check the last written scene for open loops (e.g., Is Zilver still injured? Is he still hungry?).
Report: specific "Continuity Alerts" before suggesting new text.
Example: "Alert: Last session ended with Zilver losing his shoes. Ensure he is barefoot in this scene."


Source Document 03: Setting & The Mechanics of Awakening
I. The Era: The Great Awakening
Context: A standard Medieval Fantasy setting (Castles, Horses, Swords, Feudalism), but with a biological twist.
The Threshold: For millennia, the human brain was too primitive to handle the Ethos Field. Magic was effectively non-existent because no one had the hardware to run the software.
Current State: A biological tipping point has been reached. A small percentage of the population (The Crew, Cygnus, the Cultists) possess the neural density to process the Field.
Public Perception: Magic is considered a myth, a superstition, or a symptom of madness. "Witchcraft" is feared not because it's powerful, but because it's associated with unexplained fires and sudden deaths (Brain Burnout).
II. The Status of Magic Users (The "Accident" Rule)
1. The Crew (Unconscious Casters)
Rule: No one knows they are a Mage.
They believe they are just "lucky," "tough," or "good with animals."
Magic manifests only under Extreme Stress or Deep Emotion.
The Mechanic:
Elara: Doesn't cast "Speak with Animals." She just sits quietly, and birds land on her because she feels safe. She thinks she just likes birds.
Silas: Doesn't cast "Iron Skin." He braces for a punch, expecting it to hurt. When it doesn't, he assumes the guard had a weak arm or hit a bony spot.
Mara: Doesn't cast "Ignite." She gets furious at a locked door, bangs her fist on it, and the old rusty lock mechanism "happens" to snap from heat stress. She thinks she just hit it hard.
Kael: Doesn't cast "Time Skip." He runs from guards, panicking. He thinks he barely made the gap. In reality, he arrived 0.5 seconds before he jumped.
2. Zilver (The Director)
Role: He is the only one who suspects a pattern, though he attributes it to "Logic" and "Physics" rather than Magic.
Method: He treats his crew like chess pieces. He positions them where their "natural quirks" (magic) will trigger.
Example: He puts Silas in the front not because Silas is a wizard, but because "Silas is really lucky in fights."
3. Cygnus (The Brute Force Scientist)
Status: He is the only one actively trying to use Magic, but he is doing it wrong.
The Misconception: Cygnus believes Magic is about Power. He thinks he needs more energy (Batteries/Cultists) to force reality to bend.
The Reality: He is unaware of the Flow/Saint mechanic. He is trying to push a square peg through a round hole with a sledgehammer.
The Climax: He only solves the equation when he watches Zilver merge with the water in the final scene.
III. Technology vs. "Magic"
Zilver often solves problems with Science that appears to be Magic to the medieval population.
The Lever/Pulley: The King's Obelisk. The court thinks Zilver chanted a spell to lift the stone. Zilver actually just calculated the fulcrum and used a compound pulley system.
Chemistry: Zilver's "Potions" are often just basic chemical reactions (e.g., creating smoke bombs or flash powder) enriched by his subconscious magic.
The Contrast: This blurs the line for the reader and the characters. Is it Magic? Is it Science? It's usually both.
IV. Timeline of the Story
The Inciting Incident: Zilver's accidental "Bush Fire" (The Synaptic Crash). The Letter arrives.
The Gathering: Zilver flees the capital (post-frame job) and unknowingly recruits the Outcasts (The Crew) based on their specific survival skills.
The Training (The Heists):
The Crew undertakes "Jobs" to survive/clear Zilver's name.
Each job stresses them. The stress causes Neural Hypertrophy (Growth).
Zilver begins to rely on their "Luck."
The Climax (The Water Room):
Logic fails. Science fails.
The Awakening: Zilver consciously chooses to bypass Logic and use Will.
The Witness: Cygnus watches Zilver manipulate the water without a battery/sacrifice. Cygnus realizes: "I don't need more power. I need less resistance."
Source Document 02: Character Profiles & Dynamics
I. The Protagonist
Name: [[Zilver]] (Alias: Merlin)
Archetype: The Unconscious Saint / The Architect.
Affinity: Universal (Saint Variance).
Note: He is a "Prism." He has no internal filter, allowing him to access all frequencies (Heat, Time, Bio, Force) with 1x Cost, provided his intent is altruistic.
The Mechanic: Subconscious Calculation ("Fool's Luck").
Zilver does not "cast" spells consciously (yet). His brain analyzes a problem and the Ethos Field edits reality to fit his solution.
Visual: He enters a Synaptic Lock (The Trance). He freezes, pupils dilate, breathing stops.
The Cost: Massive calorie depletion. He suffers from acute hypoglycemia (The Crash) after every major feat.
Motivation: To prove his innocence, save his friends, and solve the ultimate puzzle (The Origin of Magic).
II. The Antagonist
Name: [[High Steward Cygnus]]
Archetype: The Tyrant / The Dark Mirror.
Affinity: Universal (Tyrant Variance).
The Mechanic: Parasitic Conductance.
Cygnus forces the Ethos Field to obey. To bypass the fatal Synaptic Load of high-level magic, he uses "Batteries" (Cultists) to shoulder the cost.
Visual: Wears pristine White and Silver. Seemingly ageless. When he casts, people around him get nosebleeds or faint.
The Goal: To rewrite history. He believes the world is flawed and he is the only one with the "Vision" to fix it (by becoming King/God).
III. The Crew (The Specialists)
These characters represent the specific Affinities (Colors of Magic). They are "Citizens" with high potential in one specific area.
1. Elara (The Mute / "Mouse")
Affinity: Biomatics (Green Logic).
Heist Role: The Radar / Surveillance.
Mechanic: Sensory Bonding. She bonds with small animals (rats/birds) to see through their eyes. She can read the micro-expressions and heart rates of guards to detect lies or patrols.
Trait: Mute due to trauma. Extremely expressive eyes. Begins malnourished/dirty, reveals a unique beauty as she heals. Eventually speaks only critical intel.
Future Title: The Origin of Bonding.
2. Kael (The Outcast)
Affinity: Chrono-Kinetics (Violet Logic).
Heist Role: The Ghost / Infiltration.
Mechanic: Temporal Skipping. He doesn't turn invisible; he steps "between" seconds. He exists in the blind spots of human perception.
Trait: Asian descent, treated as invisible by society. Uses this social invisibility to fuel his magical invisibility. Quiet, efficient, cynical.
Future Title: The Origin of Time.
3. Mara (The Mother)
Affinity: Thermodynamics (Red Logic).
Heist Role: The Distraction / Demolitions.
Mechanic: Thermal Manipulation. She can boil a pot of soup instantly (Hearth) or melt a lock/cause a black powder keg to ignite (Fury). Her body temperature runs naturally high.
Trait: Voluptuous, protective, maternal. Not an outcast, but "othered." Fiercely defends the crew.
Future Title: The Origin of Energy.
4. Silas (The Monk)
Affinity: Kinetic Reinforcement (Gold Logic).
Heist Role: The Shield / Muscle.
Mechanic: Density Shifting. He can harden specific patches of skin to the density of iron to block blows. He can increase his mass to become an immovable object.
Trait: Elderly Black man, fit physique, shaved head (white hair). Gentle personality, violent capability. Meditates to manage his Synaptic Load.
Future Title: The Origin of Force.
IV. The Supporting Cast
1. Dirk
Role: The Anchor.
Function: Zilver’s connection to the mundane world. Dirk is not magic. He reminds the reader (and Zilver) of the stakes for normal people. He provides the resources/hideouts initially.
2. Melanie
Role: The Healer (Traditional).
Function: Represents "Old Medicine" vs. Zilver's "New Magic." She fixes the physical damage Zilver does to himself. A grounded, wise mother figure.
3. King Daveon
Role: The Puppet.
Function: A illiterate, boisterous King who is easily manipulated by Cygnus. He isn't evil, just incompetent and obsessed with his legacy (The Obelisk).

Source Document 01: The Arcane Physics (Magic System)
I. Core Concept
Magic in this world is not mystical; it is a Background Field of Physics (The Ethos Field) that interacts with the Human Nervous System. It follows the laws of thermodynamics and conservation of energy.
II. The Three Immutable Laws
1. The Law of Source (The Ethos Field)
Definition: An omnipresent energy field containing all frequencies of reality (Time, Heat, Gravity, Life).
Access: The human brain acts as a transceiver. Evolution has only recently produced prefrontal cortexes dense enough to process this signal without immediate death.
2. The Law of Cost (Synaptic Load)
Fuel: Magic burns Glucose and Neural Plasticity.
The Sensation: Casting feels like solving a complex equation while sprinting.
The Limit (Burnout): Overuse causes nosebleeds, migraines, tremors, hypoglycemia (The "Crash"), and eventually stroke/death.
Recovery: Requires high-calorie food and sleep to repair micro-fractures in the neural pathways.
3. The Law of Impedance (Signal Clarity)
The Citizen (High Resistance): 90% of people. Their intent is "noisy" (mixed selfish/selfless desires). Magic is hard, inefficient, and exhausting. They require specific "Affinities" (Filters) to cast at all.
The Saint (Superconductor): Total surrender of Ego (Altruism). Zero Resistance. They "ask" the field to cooperate.
Advantage: Low Cost, High Control.
Disadvantage: Sensory Backlash. They feel the physical consequences of their magic (e.g., they feel the heat of the fire they create).
The Tyrant (High Voltage): Total weaponization of Ego (Selfishness). Forced Conductance. They "command" the field to obey.
Advantage: Massive Power, No Sensory Pain.
Disadvantage: Extremely High Cost (burns out batteries/people quickly).
III. The Four Frequencies (Affinities)
Every brain has a natural "Lens" that filters the field. Casting outside your affinity increases Synaptic Load by 300-400%.
Thermodynamics (Red Logic):
Domain: Energy transfer. Fire, Ice, Lightning.
Function: Adding or removing heat.
Character Prototype: Mara (The Mother/Fury).
Kinetic Reinforcement (Gold Logic):
Domain: Force, Vector manipulation, Density.
Function: Making things harder, lighter, stopping momentum, physical shielding.
Character Prototype: Silas (The Monk).
Biomatics (Green Logic):
Domain: Cellular communication, Neural bridging.
Function: Healing (accelerating mitosis), Bonding with animals, Sensing life.
Character Prototype: Elara (The Mute Girl).
Chrono-Kinetics (Violet Logic):
Domain: Causal editing, Time, Probability.
Function: "Skipping" seconds, localized rewinds, seeing echoes of the past. Rare and dangerous.
Character Prototype: Kael (The Outcast).
Note: Zilver (Merlin) is a Saint-Class Universalist. He has no filter. He can access all four, but he thinks it is just "Luck" or "Logic." Note: Cygnus is a Tyrant-Class Universalist. He forces all four using human batteries (Cultists).
IV. The Mechanics of Casting
Logic-Based: You do not recite words. You must Visualize the Causal Chain. (e.g., To light a candle, you don't say "Fire"; you visualize the friction of molecules in the wick).
Somatic Components: Hand waves/wands are just "Mental Crutches" to help the user focus. They are not required but help reduce Synaptic Load for beginners.
The "Safety Rail": There is none. If you cast a fireball in a gas station and forget the gas, you die.
V. Training (Progression)
Method: Progressive Overload.
Process: Casting to failure -> Micro-fractures in brain -> Rest/Food -> Neural Hypertrophy (Increased Capacity).
Mastery: A "Master" is not someone who knows more spells; it is someone with the Endurance to channel high-voltage currents without passing out.
